Readings are relative (dBFS from the mic), not calibrated sound pressure — use them for pass/fail and before/after comparisons. Sweep: turn on Max hold, play one full sweep, and the orange line traces that speaker's response. Save it as a reference to compare other speakers. Pink noise: use RTA view; a healthy full-range speaker shows a fairly even, slowly falling line with no deep holes. Polarity: set a known-good speaker as reference, then check the others — "REVERSED" means a wiring swap.
